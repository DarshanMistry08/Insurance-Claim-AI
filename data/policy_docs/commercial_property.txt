COMMERCIAL PROPERTY INSURANCE POLICY — BUILDING AND BUSINESS PERSONAL PROPERTY
Policy Effective: January 1, 2023 — December 31, 2027
Issuing Company: Meridian Commercial Insurance Corporation
Policy Form: CP-1 (Commercial Property Policy — Causes of Loss Broad Form)

==============================================================
BUILDING AND STRUCTURE COVERAGE
==============================================================

CLAUSE COMMERCIAL_BUILDING_A
Building Coverage
The Company will pay for direct physical loss to buildings described in the Declarations caused by a covered cause of loss. Coverage limit: $2,000,000 per building per occurrence. Coverage includes permanent fixtures, machinery, and equipment that is part of the building. Outdoor signs attached to the building: included up to $10,000.

==============================================================
BUSINESS PERSONAL PROPERTY
==============================================================

CLAUSE COMMERCIAL_CONTENTS_B
Business Personal Property
Coverage applies to business personal property owned by the insured and located in or on the described building, including furniture, fixtures, machinery, equipment, stock, and improvements. Limit: $500,000. Property of others in the insured's care, custody, or control: included up to $25,000. Electronic data and software: covered up to $50,000.

==============================================================
WEATHER AND NATURAL PERILS
==============================================================

CLAUSE STORM_COMMERCIAL_E
Windstorm, Hail, and Weather — Commercial
Direct loss or damage to covered property caused by windstorm, tornado, hurricane, or hail is covered up to $200,000 per occurrence for commercial buildings. Damage to outdoor property, signs, awnings, and equipment not in a building is limited to $10,000. Roof inspections within 3 years of policy inception are required for full wind coverage.

CLAUSE FLOOD_COMMERCIAL_F
Commercial Flood Coverage
Unlike residential policies, this commercial policy INCLUDES limited flood coverage with a sub-limit of $100,000 per occurrence for direct flood damage to the building and its contents when caused by an overflow of an adjacent waterway. External flood deductible: $5,000. This coverage requires a separate flood endorsement; if the endorsement is not attached, flood is excluded.

==============================================================
BUSINESS INTERRUPTION
==============================================================

CLAUSE BUSINESS_INTERRUPTION_G
Business Income and Extra Expense
The Company will pay for the actual loss of business income sustained due to the necessary suspension of operations during the period of restoration caused by a covered loss. Maximum indemnity period: 12 months. Extra expense limit: $50,000. Coverage begins 72 hours after the date of the direct physical loss.

==============================================================
COMMERCIAL POLICY CONDITIONS
==============================================================

CLAUSE COMMERCIAL_CLAIM_REPORTING
Commercial Claim Reporting
Losses must be reported to the Company within 30 days of discovery (vs. 60 days for personal lines). The insured must: (a) take immediate steps to protect property; (b) provide a complete sworn statement of loss within 60 days of request; (c) cooperate fully with the adjuster. Delays in reporting may affect coverage.

CLAUSE COMMERCIAL_DEDUCTIBLE
Commercial Deductible Schedule
Standard deductible: $2,500 per occurrence. Wind/hail deductible: 2% of insured value with a minimum of $5,000. Flood deductible (if endorsed): $5,000. The deductible is applied per occurrence regardless of the number of buildings or items affected.

CLAUSE COMMERCIAL_VALUATION
Valuation and Coinsurance
Buildings are insured at replacement cost value (RCV) provided the insured maintains coverage equal to at least 80% of the full replacement cost. Failure to meet the coinsurance requirement will result in a proportional reduction in claim payment. Business personal property is valued at actual cash value (ACV) unless a replacement cost endorsement is attached.
