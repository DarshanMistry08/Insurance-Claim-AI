# HOMEOWNERS INSURANCE POLICY SPECIMEN

## 1. COVERAGE LIMITS

A. DWELLING COVERAGE
The maximum coverage for damages to the primary dwelling structure is $500,000.

B. WATER DAMAGE
Damage caused by accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from within a plumbing, heating, air conditioning, or automatic fire protective sprinkler system, or from within a household appliance, is covered up to a limit of $25,000 per incident. Water damage from external flooding is excluded unless a separate flood insurance rider is attached.

C. FIRE DAMAGE
Damage caused by fire or lightning is covered up to the full dwelling coverage limit of $500,000. Coverage includes smoke damage and damage caused by firefighters during the extinguishing process.

D. THEFT AND VANDALISM
Loss of personal property due to theft or vandalism is covered up to a limit of $15,000 per incident. High-value items such as jewelry, art, and electronics require a separate appraisal and rider for full coverage.

## 2. POLICY PERIOD
This policy is effective from January 1, 2023, to December 31, 2026. Claims for incidents occurring outside this period will be denied.

## 3. CLAIM REPORTING
All claims must be reported within 60 days of the incident date. Claims reported after this period may be denied due to late notice.

## 4. DEDUCTIBLE
A standard deductible of $1,000 applies to all approved claims unless otherwise specified in the policy declaration page. The deductible will be subtracted from the final payout amount.
